What is WebWriter?

WebWriter is a free, open-source editor for interactive learning materials. Bring writing, images, video, tables, and mathematical notation into one document, and add widgets where learners should take part.

Start with an idea for a lesson. Combine an explanation with media and an activity; work on the material alone or together. No registration is required.

Make room for exploration

Widgets add activities that learners can explore, answer, and experiment with. The collection spans science, mathematics, languages, history, and more.

Explore simulations, maps, coding, timelines, and creative tools. Choose an interaction that supports your topic, then place it alongside the text and media learners need.

Learn together

Use WebWriter to work on learning materials alone or with others. In live sessions, learners can participate while you observe their progress in real time.

Plan how people will take part: what should they explore or answer, and what will you watch for? Connect the activity to the explanation in the same document.

Build your own widgets

Developers can extend WebWriter with web components and their preferred library. Begin with a hello widget, then follow the quiz tutorial through rendering, state, and collaboration.

Have an idea that the existing collection does not cover? Use the widget documentation as a starting point for bringing that interaction into the page.
